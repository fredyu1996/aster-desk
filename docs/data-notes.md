# Aster Desk — data notes (Nov 2026)

How the 3D trading workstation (Aster-Desk.html) gets its data, and what was verified live.

## Access
- REST base: https://fapi.asterdex.com (Binance-style). CORS is `*`, **but requests carrying a foreign Referer are rejected** — fetch with `referrerPolicy: 'no-referrer'` works from any page / local file (verified from a null/file-like origin in Fred's Chrome).
- Exception: `/fapi/v1/fundingRate` (funding *history*) is blocked cross-site — only readable on Aster's own domain. Current funding (`premiumIndex`) and `fundingInfo` (interval per coin: 1h / 4h / 8h) work cross-site.
- Stream: wss://fstream.asterdex.com/stream works cross-origin.
- Rate limit: 2400 request-weight / min. The desk stays under ~400/min.
- claude.ai hosted artifacts and the Claude app's file preview block outside network calls — the desk must be downloaded and opened directly in Chrome (or served from GitHub Pages). The page shows a red warning when no data arrives.
- Background tabs: Chrome throttles timers to ~1/min; the desk runs its clock in a Web Worker so tracking continues when the tab is hidden/minimised (not if the computer sleeps).
- Claude's cloud workspace: fapi.asterdex.com added to allowed domains (Settings › Capabilities) on Nov 7 — reachable from scheduled runs. Unlock trackers (DefiLlama API, Tokenomist, CryptoRank, CoinGecko) are NOT reachable from the workspace shell; research goes through web search.
- Fees (base tier, USDT perps): 0% maker / 0.04% taker per side.

Type any Aster perp. OI, candles (15m + 1h) and last 1000 trades fetched immediately. Top card = **Reversal read**: the Trader's 8 signs, trend state, unlock line, and whether the Trader would take it now (or the exact skip reason from its gate). ## Pattern tags (top 10 each side, from 72h of 1h candles + funding/4h)
Gainers (first match wins): **Overheated** funding ≥ 0.08%/4h, or ≥15% upper wick + ≥15% off high + net selling · **Short squeeze** funding ≤ −0.03%/4h · **Crash bounce** ≥25% drop 12–72h ago and ≥20% off that low · **Fresh breakout** volume ≥2.5× previous day, net inflow, within 12% of high · else **Momentum run**.
Losers: **Shorts crowded** funding ≤ −0.03%/4h · **Trapped longs** funding ≥ +0.03%/4h · **Fading after pump** ≥30% pump 12–72h ago and volume ≤0.6× · **Capitulation** volume ≥2.5×, net selling, bottom 20% of range · **Thin bleed** < $250K/day and little net flow · else **Downtrend**.

## Day tracker / reversal watch (GainerLoser panel, first tab)
- Auto-tracks every coin entering top-5 gainers or losers for 24h (plus manual adds); stores tag time/price, 5-min snapshots, state-change log in localStorage. Each card shows the coin's unlock situation.
- 8 reversal checks every ~15s. Gainers (top signs): lower high on 15m · below 15m EMA20 · net flow negative last 1h · taker B/S < 0.9 · ≥5% upper wick last 3h · funding ≥ 0.05%/4h · OI not backing the rally · 1h RSI rolling over from >70. Losers mirror (higher low, above EMA, flow positive, B/S > 1.1, ≥5% lower wick, funding ≤ −0.03%/4h, OI covering/flush, RSI turning up from <30).
- States: Still rising/falling (0–2) · Losing steam (3–4) · Reversal forming (5+) · Reversed (5+ and ≥8% off the 24h extreme on the other side of the EMA). Alerts on every change; reversal-forming/reversed also beep and (optional) desktop notification.

## Momentum strategy (added Nov 8)
- **Momentum read** (`momRead`, track.js) for the top-10 gainers/losers and tracked coins, the mirror image of the reversal read. Gainer → LONG with the move: fresh 15m high within 3% of the 24h high · above a rising 15m EMA20 · net flow positive 1h · taker B/S > 1.1 · last-hour volume ≥ 1.3× prior hours · funding < 0.05%/4h (not crowded) · OI rising with price · 1h RSI 55–82 and not falling. Loser → SHORT: the mirror (fresh low, below a falling EMA, flow negative, B/S < 0.9, volume expanding, funding > −0.03%/4h, OI rising as price falls, RSI 18–45). States: No momentum (0–3) · Momentum building (4–5) · Strong momentum (6+). GainerLoser alerts when a mover turns strong.
- **Trader** (`strat: 'mom'`): enters on Strong momentum with ≥ `momMinScore` (6) signs. Skipped when the move is > `momMaxExt` (8%) from the 15m EMA (no chasing), when reversal signs are already forming (reversal state ≥ forming), volume < $300K, the unlock guard applies (no longs before a big unlock; no fresh shorts 24 h after), or the coin is cooling down. Risk `momRiskPct` 0.75%, max `momMaxPos` 2 momentum positions within the shared max 3. Stop beyond the last 2h swing or the EMA20 (whichever gives more room) + stop buffer. Same partial (+1.5R), breakeven, EMA trail and +3R target. Exits after 30 min if momentum drops to "No momentum" ("momentum faded") or reversal signs reach "forming" ("reversal signs forming against the move"); time stop `momHoldH` 6 h. Each strategy has its own switch (`stratRev`, `stratMom`).
- **Reviewer**: existing rules are learned from and applied to reversal trades only. Momentum rule: ≥5 momentum trades averaging < −0.25R → +1 sign, size ×0.75 (≥8 and < −0.6R → +2, ×0.5); the Trader caps momentum at 7/8. Groups: by strategy, by strategy+side. Grade D note for momentum fades: "chased a move that was already ending".
- **UI**: Momentum column in GainerLoser tables, "rev x/8 · mom y/8" on the mover screens, momentum chip on tracker cards, a Momentum read card in the coin check, strategy tags in the scan, positions, trade log and position wall, strategy switches and momentum settings under Trader → Rules & settings.
- Tested Nov 8: synthetic breakout → 8/8 → momentum long, risk 0.75%; decisive fade below the EMA → "momentum faded" exit at −0.77R; a crash through the stop → −1.04R. Live dry run on the real account: momentum short PONS 8/8; CASHCAT and ANSEM skipped as too stretched (−9%, +17% from the EMA); thin coins skipped on volume.

- Exits: half at +1.5R (setting) then stop to entry and trail with 15m EMA20; all at +3R (setting); early exit if signals fade (state back to still rising/falling after 30 min); 12 h time stop; longs closed 12 h before a big unlock. Daily loss limit 3% pauses new entries until the next day. All adjustable in Rules & settings; reset to $1,000 available.
- Each open position keeps the partial/target/hold values it was opened with; rule changes apply to new entries only. Closed trades record signs at entry, stage (forming/reversed), stop distance, Reviewer lessons in force and unlock context.

## Reviewer — trade review desk (key 5), added Nov 7
- Loop: Trader closes a trade → Reviewer grades it (A full plan worked · B small win · C scratch or clean ≈1R loss · D avoidable: stopped <45 min, gave back ≥1R, faded before confirmation · F lost >1.3R). Stop-outs are re-checked 4–22 h later: did price reach +1.5R anyway?
- Review every 3 closed trades (setting), or after 12 h with any new trade, or "Run review now". Uses the last 40 trades, excluding trades you closed by hand. Breaks results down by side, setup tag, entry stage, signs at entry, exit type, unlock at entry.
- Possible rule changes, each with minimum sample: overall avg < −0.25R over ≥6 trades → +1 sign and risk ×0.75 (≥10 trades and < −0.5R → +2 and ×0.5) · one side avg < −0.4R over ≥4 → that side needs +1 sign (≥6 and < −0.6R → half size) · tag avg ≤ −0.5R and ≤34% win over ≥3 → skip that tag; ≥ +0.5R → take it first · "forming" entries losing over ≥4 → enter only at "Reversed" · ≥25% of losers were +1R first → half off at +1.2R (≥35% → +1R) · ≥30% hit target → target +1R further; winners never reach target → target ≈ their average best R · stop-outs <45 min ≥40% (≥3) or ≥2 stopped trades later hit +1.5R → double stop buffer (max 2%) · ≥3 time stops ~flat → hold ×0.67 (min 4 h) · fees ≥35% of gross wins → 2× min volume · funding paid ≥20% of losses → skip sides paying ≥0.05%/4h · ≥3 longs with a big unlock ahead averaging ≤ −0.4R → no longs within 7 days of a big unlock.
- Each review is recomputed from the evidence, so rules relax back to your settings when results recover. It never raises risk above your setting.
- Trader's read-back: pauses entries ~20 s (immediately in the cloud), then accepts each change, adjusts it, or holds it. It caps the sign requirement at 7/8 and holds a size increase until its last 3 trades are net positive. It reruns the last 24 h of reversal candidates under old vs new rules, then writes "My plan for the next entries" (incl. the unlock rule). Lessons card shows on the Trader's Account tab; skip reasons in its scan name the lesson.
- State in localStorage (`awx_rv`); toggle adjustments off, clear lessons, change review frequency on the Reviewer's History & settings tab.
